Mindspan for agilon

Clinical workflow for agilon PCP partners

Six steps from PCP referral to care plan delivery, with the triggers that bring a member back to Mindspan. Source: Agilon proposal, Clinical Workflow and Triggers for PCP Re-Engagement.

PCP practice Mindy, virtual clinician Mindspan specialist Mindspan testing
Re-engagement and annual cycle
  1. 1

    Cognitive concern identified and referred

    PCP, at the Annual Wellness Visit

    The PCP identifies cognitive risk and refers to Mindspan. No complex intake is required from the practice.

  2. 2

    Pre-visit MoCA

    Mindy, completed by the member at home

    Mindy delivers the MoCA through a guided conversation and produces a structured cognitive risk score that guides the visit, saving about 30 minutes in the office.

    No charge to the plan or member
  3. 3

    Initial specialist assessment

    Neurologist or APP, in person

    Focused exam supported by the Mindy report. DSM-5 differential diagnosis with etiology and staging. The specialist decides whether testing is needed.

    CPT 99205
  4. 4

    Neuropsychological testing, when indicated

    Mindspan, virtual or in person

    One round of formal testing for atypical presentations, mixed etiology, mild or unclear impairment, or to separate dementia from depression or delirium. Not every member needs this step.

    CPT 96132/96133, 96136/96137 (if in scope), 96138/96139
  5. 5

    Follow-up visit and confirmed diagnosis

    Neurologist, 3 to 4 weeks after the initial visit

    Diagnosis confirmed, etiology established, and coded to HCC 125, 126 or 127. Cognitive assessment and care planning completed when indicated.

    CPT 99214/99215, plus 99483 when indicated
  6. 6

    Care plan delivered to the PCP

    PCP co-signs and becomes the longitudinal home

    Includes HCC documentation and dual coding, medication management, safety planning and caregiver guidance. Mindspan stays available for consultation, and the cycle repeats annually.

Triggers for PCP re-engagement

Between annual cycles, the PCP re-refers the member to Mindspan when any of these occur.

Caregiver-reported declineNew or worsening changes in memory, behavior or function
Acute utilizationAn ED visit or hospitalization
Care environment changeA move, a new living setting or a change in caregiving capacity
PCP-observed changeChanges noted during routine primary care

agilon authorization: Steps 2 to 6 are covered by one approved diagnostic bundle per member. Re-engagement visits and anything beyond the bundle route to agilon for approval.

Mindspan, draft for internal review. Not for distribution without approval.
